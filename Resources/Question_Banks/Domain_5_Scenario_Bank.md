# Domain 5 Scenario Bank (Program Management and Oversight)

1. A policy says "use strong passwords" without requirements. What is missing?
   Answer: Supporting standard.
   Rationale: Policy intent needs enforceable specifics.

2. Vendor contract lacks breach-notification timeline. Why is this risky?
   Answer: Delayed incident awareness and response obligations.
   Rationale: Notification SLA is key for coordinated response.

3. A control costs $8,000 yearly and reduces ALE from $20,000 to $9,000. Is it justified?
   Answer: Yes, likely justified.
   Rationale: Expected annual loss reduction exceeds control cost.

4. Security awareness completion is high, but phishing click rate remains high. What does this indicate?
   Answer: Training effectiveness problem.
   Rationale: Completion does not equal behavior change.

5. Which document should define exception approval process and expiry?
   Answer: Procedure tied to policy governance.
   Rationale: Operational detail belongs in procedures.

6. A business accepts a high risk due to operational constraints. What must still occur?
   Answer: Formal risk acceptance with owner approval and review date.
   Rationale: Acceptance requires governance and accountability.

7. Audit requests proof of access reviews. What evidence is best?
   Answer: Review logs, reviewer decisions, and remediation actions.
   Rationale: Demonstrates control operation over time.

8. Third-party hosts critical data and uses subprocessors without notice. Which contract clause is needed?
   Answer: Subprocessor disclosure and approval requirements.
   Rationale: Maintains visibility and downstream risk control.

9. Which metric better supports executive reporting: tool alerts generated or risk reduction trend?
   Answer: Risk reduction trend.
   Rationale: Outcome-focused metric aligns with leadership decisions.

10. Why should awareness training be role-based?
    Answer: Threat exposure differs by role.
    Rationale: Relevance improves retention and behavior change.

11. A control exception has no expiration date. What governance risk does this create?
    Answer: Permanent unmanaged risk acceptance.
    Rationale: Exceptions require review cadence and closure criteria.

12. Which document should define mandatory encryption algorithm requirements?
    Answer: Standard.
    Rationale: Standards specify enforceable technical requirements.

13. Why is board-level reporting of only alert volume weak?
    Answer: It lacks business risk context.
    Rationale: Leadership needs outcome and risk trend metrics.

14. Which third-party clause best supports investigation rights after breach?
    Answer: Right-to-audit and incident cooperation obligations.
    Rationale: Contracts must ensure evidentiary and response access.

15. What should follow recurring policy violations in one department?
    Answer: Targeted awareness plus control/process redesign.
    Rationale: Repeat violations indicate systemic issue, not only user behavior.

16. Which risk treatment is being used when cyber insurance is purchased?
    Answer: Risk transfer.
    Rationale: Financial exposure is shifted contractually.

17. Why must risk acceptance include named owner approval?
    Answer: Accountability for residual risk decisions.
    Rationale: Governance requires explicit authority and ownership.

18. What evidence best supports completion of security awareness campaign effectiveness?
    Answer: Reduced phishing failure rates over time.
    Rationale: Behavioral outcome beats attendance-only metrics.

19. Which GRC action is most appropriate after major regulation change?
    Answer: Control mapping review and policy updates.
    Rationale: Compliance posture must track legal changes.

20. A vendor stores regulated data but refuses breach SLA terms. Best response?
    Answer: Escalate risk and reconsider vendor approval.
    Rationale: Contractual gaps can create unacceptable compliance exposure.

21. Which metric best supports vulnerability program governance?
    Answer: Percent of critical findings remediated within SLA.
    Rationale: Measures timeliness against defined expectations.

22. Why is document version control essential in policy management?
    Answer: It ensures traceability and approved change history.
    Rationale: Auditability depends on controlled revision records.

23. What should be reviewed during annual policy cycle besides wording?
    Answer: Control effectiveness and exception trends.
    Rationale: Policies should reflect operational reality and risk outcomes.

24. Which awareness format is usually most effective for busy technical teams?
    Answer: Role-based micro-learning with short periodic drills.
    Rationale: Frequent relevant content improves retention.

25. Which contract term addresses unseen downstream processor risk?
    Answer: Subprocessor disclosure and change notification.
    Rationale: Maintains visibility into indirect data handlers.

26. What is a common distractor in governance questions?
    Answer: Technical control choice when prompt asks for governance artifact.
    Rationale: Match answer type to question scope.

27. Why should KRIs accompany KPIs in executive dashboards?
    Answer: Performance and risk indicators together provide balanced decision context.
    Rationale: KPI alone may hide emerging exposure.

28. Which action best strengthens audit readiness for IAM controls?
    Answer: Periodic access review evidence with remediation tracking.
    Rationale: Demonstrates control operation and closure discipline.

29. If ALE remains higher than control cost after mitigation, what is implied?
    Answer: Additional control investment may be justified.
    Rationale: Further reduction can still be economically beneficial.

30. What is the strongest final output of a mature security program?
    Answer: Measurable risk reduction aligned with business objectives.
    Rationale: Program success is outcome-driven, not activity-driven.
