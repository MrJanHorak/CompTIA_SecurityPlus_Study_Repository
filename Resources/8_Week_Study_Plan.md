# 8-Week Study Plan (Zero-to-Hero)

## Phase Plan

| Weeks | Focus                                   | Deliverable                             |
| ----- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 1-2   | Domain 1 + networking/security baseline | Foundation notes + first flashcard deck |
| 3-4   | Domain 2 deep dive                      | Threat and mitigation mapping drills    |
| 5     | Domain 3 architecture                   | Architecture scenario practice          |
| 6     | Domain 4 operations                     | SIEM and IR-focused hands-on labs       |
| 7     | Domain 5 oversight                      | Risk calculations and governance drills |
| 8     | Final review                            | 3 timed mocks + weak-area remediation   |

## Weekly Routine Template

- Day 1: Learn new objectives
- Day 2: Active recall + flashcards
- Day 3: Learn new objectives
- Day 4: Practice questions
- Day 5: Lab or scenario drill
- Day 6: Timed mini exam
- Day 7: Error review and update notes
