# Flashcards Guide for Security+ SY0-701

## Why Flashcards Work

Security+ includes a high density of terminology, protocols, and scenario patterns. Daily active recall is essential.

## Recommended Deck Structure

- Security+::Acronyms
- Security+::Ports and Protocols
- Security+::Threat Actors and Attacks
- Security+::Security Controls and Frameworks
- Security+::Incident Response and SIEM
- Security+::Risk and Compliance

## Card Templates

### Basic

- Front: What is the difference between SLE and ALE?
- Back: SLE = AV x EF. ALE = SLE x ARO.

### Scenario

- Front: User account shows impossible travel login followed by privileged action. What is your first action?
- Back: Validate alert, scope impacted account, initiate containment workflow.

## Daily Cadence

- Normal phase: 15-20 minutes/day
- Final 2 weeks: 25-35 minutes/day
