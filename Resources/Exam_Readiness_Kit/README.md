# Exam Readiness Kit

Use this kit in the final 2-3 weeks before your exam date.

## Included Files

- [Mock Exam Score Tracker](Mock_Exam_Score_Tracker.md)
- [Weak Area Remediation Tracker](Weak_Area_Remediation_Tracker.md)
- [Final 7-Day Cram Plan](Final_7_Day_Cram_Plan.md)

## Readiness Criteria

- Three consecutive mixed mocks at 80% or higher
- No severe weak areas in Domain 2 or Domain 4
- PBQ simulation average score at 80% or higher
