# Study Strategies for CompTIA Security+ SY0-701

## Strategy 1: Spaced Repetition

Use increasing review intervals after first learning:

| Review | Timing          | Activity                        |
| ------ | --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 1      | Within 24 hours | Summarize and create flashcards |
| 2      | 3 days          | Active recall without notes     |
| 3      | 7 days          | Mixed practice questions        |
| 4      | 14 days         | Domain-level review             |
| 5      | 30 days         | Timed mock validation           |

## Strategy 2: Active Recall

- Close notes and explain topic from memory
- Use short oral explanations for protocol and control selection
- Turn every section into 3 to 5 self-test prompts

## Strategy 3: Feynman Technique

Explain each concept as if teaching a beginner. If the explanation fails, revisit weak points and simplify.

## Strategy 4: Systems Thinking

Link controls across domains. Example: architecture segmentation in Domain 3 affects log signals in Domain 4 and compliance reporting in Domain 5.
